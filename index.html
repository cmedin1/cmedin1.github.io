Unifying the United States

A policy series grounded in the Constitution, built for the problems America faces today. Written by Carey Medin.

I write a policy series called Unifying the United States. It starts with the Constitution and works forward to the problems the country actually has right now.

Every issue does the same thing. It finds the bills and programs that already exist, credits what is working, and then proposes what is missing. No issue starts from a blank page, because almost nothing in American policy ever does.

There are 35 issues, 5 addenda, a master financial framework, and a running set of shorter notes on the news as it happens. All of it is free to read. This page links to every piece.
